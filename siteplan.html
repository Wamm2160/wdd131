Norte Brunch Website Plan

A responsive website plan for a Mexican torta shop in Querétaro, Mexico.

Site Name

NB

Norte Brunch

I selected this name because it is the real name of my Mexican torta business. The name is short, memorable, and communicates a casual food experience inspired by flavors from northern Mexico.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Norte Brunch website is to promote the restaurant and provide useful information to current and potential customers. The site will present the available tortas, drinks, desserts, prices, business hours, location, and contact information.

The website will also allow visitors to browse and filter menu products, save a favorite product using local storage, and submit a contact form.

Scenarios

  • What tortas, drinks, and desserts are available at Norte Brunch?
  • How much does each menu item cost?
  • Where is Norte Brunch located, and what are its business hours?
  • How can a customer contact the restaurant or save a favorite product?
